# TA Session Intro

Many of you may be wondering why this course requires a project, especially since it is a Database Management Systems (DBMS) course rather than Software Analysis & Design (SAD).

There is a long history behind this requirement, which I will skip. In brief, Prof. Chou sees it as an opportunity to practice building a project, particularly since some students may not have taken the SAD project seriously.

So SAD that you cannot do all your suffering in SAD class... just a terrible joke, of course.

We TAs understand that this is a heavy workload, especially if you have never built a full-stack project. We are here to help.

The TA sessions will teach you essential skills for building a full-stack web application.

:::important
The following is TA Harvey's personal opinion. The other TA and professor may not agree.

Setting up a development environment on your own computer is something you learn by doing.

I will share my settings and configuration. If they do not work for you, please try looking for a solution yourself first.

In many cases, we would simply be searching online in front of you anyway.

If you are still stuck, tell me what the problem is, what you have tried, and what happened each time.

Then I will do my best to help.

Good luck!
:::
